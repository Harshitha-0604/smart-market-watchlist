## Product pitch (100 words)

Most watchlists show `%change` and call it done — but a 2% move means
nothing without context. Signal replaces raw percentages with an
**attention score**: price move adjusted for each stock's own
volatility (so quiet stocks don't get drowned out by loud ones),
volume vs. session-expected volume, gap-at-open detection, and news
flags — combined into one ranked "what deserves a look right now"
feed. Stale data is flagged with an explicit confidence value instead
of silently shown as current. The board re-sorts itself by significance,
not alphabetically. Server-side "last seen" snapshots per user mean the
diff survives refreshes and devices, not just a browser's local storage.
